Our Story: A Taste of Mother’s Love

Our journey began with a very humble step and a handful of hard-earned savings. It all started in a small kitchen, where my mother used to cook the most delicious Vuna Khichuri. Inspired by her extraordinary culinary gift, a simple idea was born. In the beginning, my mother did what she does best—cooking with all her heart—while I managed the sales and served our customers. Thanks to the unmatched taste of her handmade recipes, it didn't take long for Shohel Mama's Vuna Khichuri to gain widespread popularity and love from our community.

For us, Shohel Mama's Vuna Khichuri is much more than just a food brand; it is a warm reflection of a mother’s affection and care. Every plate we serve carries the iconic, heartwarming taste of authentic home-cooked food. Our mission is simple: to share the warmth and deliciousness of a mother’s cooking with everyone.
